All five rules are evaluated on the unchanged balanced family vector. They ask different decision questions and therefore need not select the same carrier. Display order sorts scores descending and uses carrier names alphabetically for equal scores; it is not a strict preference rank. Lexical and structural therefore tie at $2/5$ under the threshold-share rule despite occupying display positions 2 and 3. Hybrid is the unique winner of that rule at $3/5$. The artifact retains the historical column name \emph{rank} for these deterministic positions.
